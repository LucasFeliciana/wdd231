Naperville Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

Naperville Chamber of Commerce

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Naperville Chamber of Commerce website is to support local businesses by providing information about events, networking opportunities, membership benefits, and community resources. The site will also help residents and visitors discover local businesses and services in Naperville.

Target Market

Local business owners, entrepreneurs, residents, and visitors in Naperville and surrounding communities.

Site Goals

  • Support local businesses by providing useful resources and networking opportunities.
  • Promote local businesses, events, and services in the Naperville community.
  • Encourage new businesses to join the Chamber of Commerce and become active in the community.

User Personas

Small Business Owner

Sarah is a local small business owner in Naperville. She is looking for networking opportunities, business resources, and ways to promote her company in the community.

Local Resident

Michael is a Naperville resident who wants to discover local businesses, services, and upcoming community events.

Entrepreneur

David is planning to start a business in Naperville. He is looking for information about the local business community, networking opportunities, and Chamber membership.

Scenarios

  • How can I join the Naperville Chamber of Commerce?
  • What local businesses are members of the Chamber?
  • What business and community events are happening in Naperville?

SEO Plan

  • Use relevant keywords related to Naperville businesses, events, and services.
  • Use clear page titles and meta descriptions for each page.
  • Use descriptive headings and meaningful content throughout the website.

Design Brief

Colors

Primary Color: Navy Blue

Secondary Color: Light Blue

Background Color: White

Text Color: Dark Gray

Typography

Font Family: Roboto

Site Map

  • Home
  • Discover
  • Directory
  • Join

Wireframes

Desktop

The desktop layout will include a header with the Chamber name and navigation menu, a main content area with information and business cards, and a footer with contact information and additional links.

Mobile

The mobile layout will use a compact header with a hamburger menu. Content and business cards will be displayed vertically for easier viewing on smaller screens.
